Dear Editors of *Database*,

We submit the manuscript entitled "TE-KG: a provenance-aware knowledge graph
and multi-layer resource for human transposable elements" for consideration as
a Database Resource article.

TE-KG addresses the practical difficulty of examining human transposable
element classification, representative sequence and genomic records,
expression, co-expression, and literature-linked biomedical relations through
one evidence-aware resource. The current runtime snapshot contains 225
transposable element nodes, 2,308 paper nodes, and 12,444 directed biological
relations with PubMed provenance. It also provides three expression contexts
covering 1,158 samples and approved co-expression catalogue entries for 285
transposable elements and 499 genes. The manuscript emphasizes the provenance
and interpretation boundary of each layer rather than treating heterogeneous
records as equivalent evidence.

We believe the resource is relevant to *Database* readers because it combines
curated, downloadable data with connected routes for browsing, graph and path
exploration, expression inspection, and source-visible natural-language
retrieval. The resource will be freely available without login at
